\chapter{State of the Art}

This chapter provides an overview of the technologies, standards, and prior work
relevant to the development of an Encrypted ClientHello (ECH) test setup for Lighttpd.
It first introduces the technical background required to understand ECH within the
TLS 1.3 protocol and then surveys work related to ECH implementations, testing
methodologies, and deployment models. The chapter concludes with a summary that
compares existing approaches and highlights the gap addressed by this dissertation.

\section{Summary}

This chapter introduced the technical foundations of TLS~1.3 and ECH, reviewed
existing implementations of ECH across various libraries, and discussed prior work
in deployment, testing, and analysis. Existing research demonstrates both the
potential and challenges of deploying ECH in real-world environments.

However, very limited work has examined ECH integration on lightweight web servers
such as Lighttpd. Most existing literature focuses on NGINX or general-purpose TLS
libraries, leaving a gap in the evaluation of smaller, embedded-friendly servers.
This dissertation addresses this gap by building a reproducible ECH testing
environment specifically for Lighttpd.
